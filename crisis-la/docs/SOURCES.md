# LA scenario and geographic anchors

## Fictional premise

The user requested an alternate version of the existing demo: a much stronger Hurricane Rachel hits Los Angeles and causes damage within the city and surrounding communities. This is an authored counterfactual, not a claim that such a landfall, intensity, or recovery operation happened. No historical date, measured wind speed, forecast probability, or hurricane category is asserted.

All damage reports, aid operations, source identities, dialogue, times, report relationships, and the school-to-stadium relocation are fictional. Current satellite imagery supplies geographical context and does not show the described damage. The relocation arc represents a relationship between sites; it is not a route or a passability assessment.

## Real geographic anchors

Addresses checked on October 17, 2026:

- [John Marshall High School official contact page](https://marshallhs.lausd.org/apps/pages/index.jsp?pREC_ID=2588321&type=d&uREC_ID=4269581): 3939 Tracy Street, Los Angeles.
- [Dodger Stadium official information](https://www.mlb.com/dodgers/ballpark/information): 1000 Vin Scully Avenue, Los Angeles.

Esri World Geocoding returned John Marshall High School at latitude 34.1079254, longitude -118.2773743 and Dodger Stadium at latitude 34.0725774, longitude -118.2402953. Both were POI matches with score 100. Coordinates describe current geography, not emergency-service data.

The school marker uses that approximate POI. The destination marker at latitude 34.0716, longitude -118.2475 is an authored parking-area anchor southwest of the stadium, not a verified entrance or actual pickup position. Named facilities do not endorse this demo and are not represented as operating real relief sites.

Other report markers are illustrative anchors in Santa Monica, Pasadena, the LA River corridor, Los Feliz, East LA, South LA, Echo Park, Elysian Park, and downtown. The fallback is an approximate diagram, not a street map.

## Media

The local MP3s are synthetic voices generated from the prepared LA script. They are not recordings of affected residents or officials. The application makes no live phone calls. Camera panels use generated photographic still recreations of generic facilities, explicitly labeled as simulated frames. There are no live CCTV feeds.
