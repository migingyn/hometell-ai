# LA prepared recording script

Fictional Hurricane Rachel exercise. Prepared Kokoro neural voices use natural speaking speed, with full captions. All times below are replay time. The last two cues require supervisor approval.

| Replay window | Role | Exact dialogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–14 s | Guide | In this fictional scenario, Hurricane Rachel has hit Los Angeles. You’re running the hotline. A caller needs water, but the pickup address may have changed. |
| 14–23 s | Caller | Hi, I need some drinking water. Is pickup still at John Marshall High? |
| 44–55 s | Guide | Some people say the school. Others say pickup has moved. Let’s call the coordinator and check which address is right. |
| 65–71.8 s | Agent | Hi, I’m checking the water pickup location for our hotline. Are you still at John Marshall High? |
| 71.8–78.6 s | Coordinator | We’ve moved to the Dodger Stadium parking lot, at 1000 Vin Scully Avenue. |
| 78.6–82.5 s | Coordinator | Flooding closed our pickup site at the school. |
| 82.5–86.3 s | Coordinator | You can get food and drinking water here. |
| 86.3–93 s | Agent | Okay, 1000 Vin Scully Avenue, instead of the school. What time do you close today? |
| 93–110 s | Coordinator | I don’t have a confirmed closing time yet. |
| 140–143.2 s | Caller | Where can I pick up drinking water? |
| 143.2–171 s | Agent | You can get food and drinking water at 1000 Vin Scully Avenue, in the Dodger Stadium parking lot. Pickup has moved from John Marshall High School. I don’t have a confirmed closing time yet. |

The number 1000 is spoken as “one thousand”; captions retain the address digits. Reply windows allow about 0.6 seconds between clips so lines can finish naturally. Longer caption holds provide reading time without stretching speech. See [audio inventory](AUDIO_ASSETS.md) for file names and reproduction.

The corrected answer is built from the approved LA record. Opening the camera inspector pauses speech along with the scenario, and closing it resumes prior playback. Muted playback retains complete captions.
