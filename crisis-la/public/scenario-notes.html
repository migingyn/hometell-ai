Hurricane Rachel · Fictional exercise

This prepared demo imagines a much stronger Hurricane Rachel striking Los Angeles, causing flooding, wind damage, and power failures across the city and nearby communities. It does not reconstruct an actual LA hurricane or predict one.

The story

A resident needs drinking water. The hotline still lists John Marshall High School, but reports suggest pickup has moved. In a scripted conversation, a coordinator explains that flooding closed the school pickup operation. A supervisor approves a new listing at the Dodger Stadium parking lot, and the next caller receives that reviewed address.

The damage, aid operations, relocation, callers, statements, and timing are invented. Neither facility is represented as providing these services in real life.

Geographic references

  • John Marshall High School official contact page — 3939 Tracy Street.
  • Dodger Stadium official information — 1000 Vin Scully Avenue.

Facility addresses were checked against official pages and Esri geocoding. The stadium marker is an approximate parking-area anchor. Other incident markers are illustrative. Present-day satellite imagery and terrain provide geographic context; they do not depict the fictional damage.

Playback and review

All voices are locally generated synthetic speech. Camera panels contain generated still-image recreations of a generic school entrance and stadium staging area, not actual CCTV or exact facility views. No live calls or model inference are involved. Capture times and visual observations are authored scenario data. The destination and next caller's answer update after supervisor approval. The arc connects two locations; it does not identify a safe travel route. Closing time stays unconfirmed.
