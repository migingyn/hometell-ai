# Heartbeat customer and payment rationale

Checked October 17, 2026. Target customer: a Medicare-certified home-health agency. Proposed buyer: administrator or clinical director; daily user: nurse/care coordinator. This is a customer hypothesis supported by an existing operational duty, not a completed customer interview or purchase commitment.

**Why the agency needs a disaster workflow.** [42 CFR §484.102(b)(1)–(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-484.102) requires individual patient disaster plans, procedures for informing state/local emergency-preparedness officials about patients needing evacuation, and follow-up with on-duty staff and patients when an emergency interrupts services. Agencies must inform state/local officials about those they cannot contact. [Accessible regulation text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/484.102).

Heartbeat's patient contact history, unanswered list, and reported ride needs can support the agency's review and reporting. A ride flag alone is not an evacuation assessment or a completed report. The current prototype does not submit reports to government, cover staff accountability, or satisfy the whole emergency-preparedness rule. Government is the coordination/report recipient in this initial customer model; the agency buys and operates the tool.

**Economic incentive, not a reimbursement claim.** Under the [CMS Expanded Home Health Value-Based Purchasing Model](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/innovation-models/expanded-home-health-value-based-purchasing-model), participating agencies' Medicare fee-for-service payments can be adjusted from **−5% to +5%**, based on quality performance relative to peers. Payment adjustments began in 2025, using 2023 performance; outcomes in a performance year affect a later payment year. The [CMS model guide](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/media/document/hhvbp-exp-model-guide) describes the 2026 adjustment based on 2024 performance.

Our hypothesis is that less repetitive calling, more complete notes, and timely nurse review are useful to these agencies. We have not demonstrated improved HHVBP scores, reduced admissions, or financial savings. HHVBP does not automatically reimburse an AI call or pay for this software.

**Pitch:** Heartbeat turns routine post-op phone check-ins into evidence-backed nursing follow-ups. During an emergency, the same roster and calling workflow help the agency find who needs assistance and who remains unreachable.
